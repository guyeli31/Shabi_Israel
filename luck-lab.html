Formula

Data input

s — prior width1.00

Smaller s = stronger shrinkage toward “on-model” (θ=0). Larger s = the data dominates.

logit(pᵢ) = ln(pᵢ / (1−pᵢ)) — fixed curve; dots = your matches

contribᵢ(θ) — the actual multiplier of the Prior (qᵢ if win, 1−qᵢ if loss)

Win: qᵢ(θ) — rises with θ Loss: 1−qᵢ(θ) — falls with θ ∏contribᵢ — combined multiplier ● dot = contribᵢ(0) = P(outcome)

Formula — three steps

1 probability → log-odds

logit(pi)= lnpi1−pi

2 shift by luck θ, then back to a probability

qi(θ)= σ(logit(pi)+θ) = 11+ e−(logit(pi)+θ)

3 likelihood of the outcome that happened

contribi(θ)= qi(θ) if win 1−qi(θ) if loss

Per-match data (θ shown symbolically)

Posterior(θ) = Prior × ∏contrib — its area shrinks; green share = D

θ>0 · lucky (D) θ<0 · unlucky Posterior Prior

Formula

Posterior(θ)∝ Prior(θ)⋅ ∏icontribi(θ)

Total combination

The Prior starts with area = 1. The data multiplies it point-by-point by ∏contrib (each ≤ 1), so the Posterior sits below the prior and its area genuinely shrinks — the more surprising the data, the smaller it gets. D is the green (θ>0) share of that shrunken area.
